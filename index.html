About the Festival

The Legend Returns

ZEAL is the annual cultural extravaganza of the St. Andrews Group of Institutions — a grand convergence of art, music, dance, drama, and competition. Inspired by the spirit of ancient Greek arenas where the best competed for glory, ZEAL is where talent meets spectacle.

Frequently Asked Questions

ZEAL is the annual cultural festival of the St. Andrews Group of Institutions. It is a multi-day celebration of art, music, dance, drama, and competition — bringing together thousands of students from across the region.

ZEAL is open to all college students. Both students of St. Andrews Group of Institutions and students from external colleges are welcome to participate in most events. Check individual event rules for specific eligibility criteria.

Yes! ZEAL warmly welcomes participants from all colleges and universities. We believe in fostering inter-college cultural exchange. You will need to carry a valid college ID card. Registration details will be announced on the Announcements page.

You can register for events directly on the day of the event at the venue, subject to seat availability. Keep an eye on the Announcements page for registration updates and deadlines.

ZEAL offers exciting prizes including cash rewards, trophies, certificates, and special goodies for winners and runners-up across all events. The overall championship team wins the prestigious ZEAL Trophy. Total prize pool exceeds ₹5 Lakhs!

Entry fees vary by event and participant type. St. Andrews students get preferential rates. Most individual events have a nominal fee of ₹100-200 per participant. Group events have team-based pricing. Check individual event pages for exact details.
